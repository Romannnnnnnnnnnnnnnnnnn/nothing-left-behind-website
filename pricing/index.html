Probate

A three-bed probate uses the three-bed house band, plus time to sort what stays. Guide £1,550–£2,400 + VAT.

We do not rush these. If items need valuing or collecting by the family first, we book around that.

What is in the price

  • Labour, loading and the van
  • Licensed waste disposal and recycling
  • A swept finish of the rooms we clear
  • Donation of usable goods where we can
  • VAT, added at the current rate
  • Difficult access, long carries or no parking
  • Hazardous items we cannot take (asbestos, gas bottles)
  • Deep cleaning beyond a swept floor

Comparisons used to set these bands include published 2025–26 lists from clearance firms around Surrey (property clearances from roughly £250 for a studio to £1,600–£2,800 for a four-bed). We have lifted those figures by about 12–15% so the guide is conservative, not a bargain claim. Longer distances may add a call-out, confirmed in the quote.

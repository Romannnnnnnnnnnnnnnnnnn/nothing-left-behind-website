What we do

A Surrey company that leaves the place empty.

We are a clearance company with a head office in Weybridge, covering Surrey and the surrounding areas. The work is straightforward: we arrive, we empty the rooms you ask us to, we take reusable items to charity where we can, and we dispose of the rest through licensed waste routes. You are left with a swept space — nothing left behind.

That covers a full house before a sale, a flat at the end of a tenancy, a garage you have not parked in for years, an office moving out, or a probate home that needs to be handled with a bit of care.

  • Full or part-property clearances
  • Fixed quotes after photos or a visit
  • Charity, reuse and licensed disposal
  • Swept finish, ready for keys to change hands

Licensed and insured

Registered waste carrier, public liability cover, and a paper trail for every load.

Reuse before the tip

Usable furniture and goods go to charity or reuse where we can. The rest is sorted, not dumped.

How it works

Three steps, then an empty room.

  1. 01

    Tell us what needs clearing

    A few photos or a short call is enough for a first figure. We can visit if the job is larger or a probate.

  2. 02

    A fixed quote, no surprises

    Labour, loading, van and licensed disposal are in the price. We only start when you are happy with the number.

  3. 03

    We empty it. You move on.

    Our crews clear the agreed rooms, sort reuse and recycling, and leave the space swept.

Services

House, flat, probate, garage and business.

Five kinds of clearance, one crew. We empty the rooms you ask us to, sort reuse from waste, and leave the place swept.

01

House clearance

Most of our work is family houses across Surrey and the surrounding towns. Crews empty living rooms, kitchens, bedrooms, lofts and sheds in a single visit where we can, sort items for reuse or charity, and leave the property swept and ready for sale, let or the next occupant.

  • Furniture, appliances and general household contents
  • Loft, shed and garden items as part of the same job
  • Donation of usable goods where possible
  • Licensed disposal of the rest, with a swept finish

02

Flat clearance

Flats need a different plan: parking, lifts, stairs and building rules. We work quickly and quietly, protect common parts, and can often complete a one-bed in a morning. End-of-tenancy, downsizing and landlord jobs are all routine.

  • Studio, one-bed and two-bed apartments
  • End of tenancy and landlord turnarounds
  • Stair and lift access planned in the quote
  • White goods, furniture and packed belongings

03

Probate clearance

A probate clearance is not a rush job. We work at the family’s pace, set aside anything that should be kept or valued, and treat the house as a private home rather than a waste collection. Executors and solicitors across Surrey and nearby get a clear quote, a booked date, and a property left ready for sale or handover.

  • Time built in to sort keep, donate and dispose
  • Discreet, fully insured teams
  • Work scheduled around legal or sale dates
  • Swept handover to family, solicitor or agent

04

Garage clearance

Garages fill up for years. We take tools, sports gear, old furniture, paint tins and general clutter, recycle what we can, and leave a clear floor. Light jobs are often done the same week; packed workshops are quoted after photos.

  • Single and double garages, sheds and workshops
  • Tools, furniture, appliances and boxed storage
  • Same-week slots for lighter loads
  • Responsible disposal of household chemicals where we can accept them

05

Business clearance

When a lease ends or a team moves, furniture and IT cannot sit around. We clear small offices through to larger open-plan floors, remove desks, chairs, cabinets and kitchen waste, and can work evenings or weekends so trading is not interrupted — from high-street units to business parks.

  • Offices, shops, clinics and industrial units
  • Desks, chairs, filing and general office waste
  • Out-of-hours visits on request
  • IT and electricals taken for licensed WEEE disposal
